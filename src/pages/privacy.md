**Effective Date:** *07/07/2024*

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to *eiilo.com*. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your personal data when you visit our website.

## 2. Data Collection

We only collect and store personal data necessary to provide our services, specifically through the contact form and the newsletter subscription.

## 3. Data We Collect

**Contact Form:** When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any other information you choose to provide.

**Newsletter Subscription:** When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

## 4. How We Use Your Data

**Contact Form:** We use the information from the contact form to respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.

**Newsletter Subscription:** We use your email address to send you our newsletter and updates.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

We ensure that all personal data is stored securely. Access to this data is limited to authorized personnel only and is protected using industry-standard security measures.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

- When required by law or to comply with a legal process.
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of *eiilo.com*, our users, or others.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your personal data.
- Withdraw consent to the processing of your data (where we are relying on consent).

## 8. No Use of Cookies

Our website does not use cookies or any other tracking technologies to collect data about your browsing activities.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on our website. Your continued use of our site following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of such changes.

This privacy policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other relevant French data protection laws. For more detailed legal requirements and guidance, consult with a legal professional.
