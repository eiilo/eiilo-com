---
title: Mood Dude is on the App Store
date: 2026-11-19
---

The three Mood Dude games are now on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. They are the first *eiilo* games there, and they are free, as they are on Google Play.

- [Mood Dude: Color Switch](https://apps.apple.com/app/mood-dude-color-switch/id6829543306) is the original. On Google Play it is simply called Mood Dude.
- [Mood Dude: Infinity](https://apps.apple.com/app/mood-dude-infinity/id6829543585) is the endless one.
- [Mood Dude: Adventures](https://apps.apple.com/app/mood-dude-adventures/id6829543664) is a platform adventure across eight island worlds.

There is more about each of them at [mooddude.app](https://mooddude.app/), and all our games are at [games.eiilo.com](https://games.eiilo.com/).
